Privacy

Last updated 2026-10-27

What we store

If you sign in with X, we receive your X user id, username, display name, profile picture URL and, if X provides it, your email address. If you pick a name instead, we store an anonymous account id and that name. We also store what you post: recipes, comments, votes and reports, with the time you posted them.

What is public

Your username or picked name, your recipes, comments, and vote totals per recipe. Who voted for what is not shown to other users. Your email address is never shown.

Where it lives

Accounts and posts are stored with Supabase (database and sign-in). The site itself is served by GitHub Pages. Your browser keeps your session and site preferences (theme, drafts) in local storage. There are no ads and no third-party analytics.

Deleting your data

You can delete your own recipes and comments on the site. To delete your account and everything attached to it, message @_lyraaaa_ on X.
